## What to carry into KCAMP

1. **C1 acceptance before code:** enumerate every route for rows6/12/18/24 and real expert histograms; identify batch-size cliffs before optimizing FLOPS. Use1369 as design evidence and1368 as warning: removing four launches can lengthen the surviving launch enough to erase the gain, and b/a rounding changed.
2. **C3 is engine work, not #1294 again:**1294 already overlaps eligible eager sends; it cannot make two complete decode microbatches independently in flight. Tie ownership of token history, speculative outcomes, PP output buffers and Engram/KV rows to a batch, then test host/GPU timelines. No new 1Cat PR in this window demonstrates exact V4.1 PP2 decode pipelining.
3. **Memory audit before C0/C5:** trace Engram800MiB transient, per-pass sparse workspaces and per-request hot-ring allocation.1342 offers exact700MiB reduction on stage0 if that transient exists;1347's generic540–560MiB saving does not apply automatically to our custom path.
4. **Retain exactness:**1358/1359 compile clone fixes,1331ties,1341prefix rings and1368batch rounding show why C2 is a separate work item. No near-tie equivalence is silently promoted to bitwise identity.

Supplementary vLLM pointers from parent (#61464/#63956/#60618/#64442/#59131/#64458) were searched against the110PR bodies; none appears by upstream number. That is **not a proof of absence of equivalent code**: inspect source/diffs against1Cat baseline before porting.

Engram source confirmation: upstream base899e9ed33 [`model.py:210–215`](https://github.com/1CatAI/1Cat-vLLM/blob/899e9ed33/vllm/models/deepseek_v41/model.py#L210-L215) runs full projected gather then per-row gate. The published series/engine patch files were searched for row-block gather equivalents and none was found. This establishes published-source opportunity, not latest deployed-memory measurement.
