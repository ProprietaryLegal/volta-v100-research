## Summary and recommendations (L-REF)

Data: 10 runs (prompts x modes x index-K variants), every prefill token and 31 greedy decode steps; max over all of them. Both numerics modes agree to within a few percent.

1. **Residual stream: BF16, never FP16.** Max 3.625e+05 (> 65,504). The extremes sit on BOS tokens — position 0 and also BOS tokens generated mid-sequence (p2_code continuation) — as on V4.
2. **hc_pre collapse** (sublayer input before RMSNorm) reaches 4.617e+05: it must be computed and held in FP32 (or BF16) until the norm; only the normed output may be FP16.
3. **MoE combine (routed + shared) and its all-reduce stay FP32** (max 2.283e+04 > 8,192). TP4 partials peak lower but the summed value does not fit the rule.
4. **Attention sublayer output may go FP16 at P5** (max 441 << 8,192).
5. **Per-expert outputs (port order, weight after w2):** max 1.502e+04 vs the 16,384 rule — allowed, but with only 8 % margin on four prompts. L-REF recommendation: keep the FP32 epilogue (a clamp would be a model change, §4.3).
6. Engram wkv output, q.k logits and indexer scores are far from any FP16 limit (tables below).
