- MEASURED. Launches sized by capacity cost almost nothing. A grid of the live segments only (the "live grid" arm)
  saves 0.003 to 0.015 ms per round at every live length. The 512K-capacity server gives the same totals within
  0.011 ms (0.145 to 1.095 ms per round, 1K to 512K). The dead blocks past the live end are not the cost.
  Verdict: under the card's 0.3 ms bar. Close the card.
- MEASURED. The larger, separate saving is the choice of kernel at short live length. The one-kernel `index_topk`
  beats the six-launch path at 1K, 4K and 16K by 0.108, 0.185 and 0.149 ms per round. From 64K up it loses
  (1.87 ms against 0.62 ms at 64K, 33.9 ms against 2.03 ms at 1M). That saving is also under 0.3 ms.
- INFERENCE. The flat 77 us per call from 16K to 128K is the time one block takes to walk one 8,192-score segment
  (k7 write alone is 27 us of it). The cost is set by the segment size SEG and by the live length, not by capacity.

## Method

- READ. Engine path (`attention_ops.py:962-1030`): a decode call (tokens x 8 heads per rank <= 64) scores the live
  keys, reduces them, then calls `index_topk_parallel` when `keys.shape[0] > 16384`, else `index_topk`.
  `keys.shape[0]` = max_len // ratio + 1 (`kv_store.py:208`): 524,289 (stage 0, ratio 2) and 1,048,577 (stage 1,
  ratio 1) at max-model-len 1,048,576, so nseg = 65 and 129. The scores are `out[:, :width]` of a buffer whose row
  stride is the bank width, roundup8(rows) (`kv_store.py:123`, `attention_ops.py:935-939`). The bench uses those widths
  and strides.
- READ and MEASURED. Rows per call: 6. The boot log `run/state/p6-boot5.log` has `num_speculative_tokens: 5` and
  `cudagraph_capture_sizes [1..6]`, so a decode round verifies 1 + 5 tokens. The decode branch admits up to 8 tokens
  (`DECODE_ROWS = 8`). The six-row figure is for one request.
- READ. Indexer layers: 8 per round, 3 on stage 0 (layers 2, 8, 14) and 5 on stage 1 (20, 24, 28, 32, 36), from
  `index_source_layer_ids` and `model.py:592`. The drafter's attention has no indexer.
- MEASURED. Inputs: the 6 rows are positions L-6 to L-1. In each row the min(n, 600) largest live values are distinct
  fp16 values in [2.5, 8) at random positions, every other live value is below 2.0, and every column past n holds
  30000. Correctness: each arm's output from a graph replay must equal torch.topk's ascending indices row by row (or
  arange(n), -1 padded, when n <= 512). The checker was first shown to reject k = 511, the head of top-513, k = 256
  against 512, a one-index mutation and a call that reads 64 columns past the live end. All 306 timed arms passed.
- MEASURED. Timing: each arm captured in a CUDA graph of 10 back-to-back calls (the engine runs these kernels inside
  the FULL decode graph). 3 s card warm-up, then 30 warm-up replays and 20 batches of 20 replays per arm, with
  `torch.cuda.synchronize()` around each batch. A CUDA event pair around each replay gives 400 samples of the mean of
  10 calls. Batch wall time agrees within 1.00 to 1.33 times the event median. The sweep ran twice in one process,
  and pass 1 is within 5 % of pass 2 except for 8 of 306 arms (the first configurations, up to 11 %).
- MEASURED. One test card, Tesla V100-SXM2-32GB-LS, 80 SMs, power limit 150 W, stock clocks
  (max SM 1,440 MHz). Peak device memory reserved 69 MB. Nothing pinned or registered. Engine module
  `index_topk.py` sha256 c491ab97806ed4cb, identical to the V100 server's engine/src copy.
- MEASURED. The first run (`topk-full-full-20261110T042023Z.*`, one call per graph, no card warm-up) is superseded.
  It counted the host gap before each graph launch (1K calls of 23 to 30 us against 1.5 to 24 us now) and the first
  configurations ran slow. Its checks also passed.

## Not measured

- The "grid of min(segments, cap) blocks" kernel does not exist. The live-grid arm stands in for it by passing only
  the live columns (`scores[:, :n_max]`). A real fixed-shape kernel would also loop, so its cost is at or above the
  live-grid figure (INFERENCE).
- the V100 server's production cards: clocks and power state there may differ from the 150 W stock-clock test card. The shares of
  the 40 to 65 ms round assume the per-call times carry over (INFERENCE).
- The prefill top-k calls (`index_topk` in `select_compressed_positions` and `index_fuse`) are not part of a decode
  round and were not timed. The scorer and the reduce kernels before the top-k were not timed.
- Interaction with neighbouring kernels in the real decode graph (L2 state, overlap) was not measured. Each arm ran
  alone on the card.
